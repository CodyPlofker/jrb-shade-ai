## Key Findings for AI Shade Matcher

### 1. Shade Distribution (this batch)
- **Fair**: 3 tickets
- **Light**: 2 tickets
- **Beige**: 8 tickets (most common)
- **Medium**: 1 ticket
- **Medium-Dark / Dark / Deep**: 0 tickets in this batch

**Critical gap**: This batch is heavily skewed toward Fair-to-Beige skin tones. The April-June 2025 data from the last page of each month (most recent 100 tickets per batch) contains NO medium-dark, dark, or deep skin tone matches. Additional data pulls targeting those shade ranges will be needed.

### 2. Agent Template Patterns
Two distinct agent styles observed:
- **Agent Samantha (ID: 4237b3ff)**: Uses a comprehensive template with full product catalog (face, eyes, lips, skin, tools). Very consistent shade-to-product mapping.
- **Agent Kaitlyn (ID: 61f5d646)**: Uses a shorter, more personalized template. Offers selfie-request-first approach. Different Face Pencil shade logic (e.g., 08/06 for Beige vs Samantha's 10/09).

### 3. Photo Requirements
Agents consistently request:
- No makeup
- Natural lighting (outside or facing a window)
- No shadows
- Chest/collarbones up
- 3-4 feet from window

### 4. Common Customer Concerns
- Rosacea / redness (Bronze MB helps combat redness)
- Dark circles (Neutralizer Pencil + lighter Face Pencil shade)
- Age spots (Face Pencil same shade as face coverage)
- Pores (WTF vs JETM decision)
- Seasonal shade shifts (Beige winter -> Medium summer)
- Cross-brand shade translation (agents prefer selfie over brand matching)

### 5. Product Logic Rules
- WTF for dry skin / winter; JETM for oily skin / summer / warm climate
- Face Pencil: always 2 shades — darker for face, lighter for under eye
- Neutralizer: always applied UNDER the Face Pencil, never alone
- Miracle Balm: agents recommend 4 shades per customer covering glow, blush, bronzing, highlighting
- Tinted Face Powder: used to reduce dewiness on top of MB/WTF
