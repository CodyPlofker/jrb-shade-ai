## Key Patterns Observed

### 1. Standard Recommendation Template
Agents follow a consistent structure:
1. **WTF/JETM shade** (always the same shade for both products)
2. **Face Pencil**: Two shades -- one for face (higher number), one for under-eye (lower number, ~2 shades lighter)
3. **Neutralizer Pencil**: Optional, for under-eye darkness or discoloration
4. **Miracle Balm**: 2-3 options offered as "skin enhancers" (not coverage)
5. **Tinted Face Powder**: To set and reduce shine
6. **Product education**: How to apply WTF, balm break-the-seal technique, etc.

### 2. Shade-to-Face-Pencil Mapping (from extracted data)
| WTF/JETM Shade | Face Pencil (Face) | Face Pencil (Under Eye) | Neutralizer |
|----------------|-------------------|------------------------|-------------|
| Fair | 05 | 03 or 04 | Fair Pink or Fair Peach |
| Light | 05 or 06 | 03 or 04 | Fair Pink |
| Beige | 09 | 07 | Light Peachy Pink |
| Medium | -- | -- | -- |
| Almond | -- | -- | -- |

| Skin Tone Range | Miracle Balm Recommendations |
|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Fair | Dusty Rose, Flushed, Pinky Bronze |
| Light | Dusty Rose, Flushed |

### 4. Photo Requirements
Agents consistently request:
- **No makeup**
- **Natural lighting** (outside or facing a window)
- **Chest up**
- **No shadows**
- Poor photos result in requests for new photos (no guessing)

### 5. Common Customer Concerns
- Rosacea / redness management (use foundation on specific areas)
- Dry skin (WTF preferred over JETM)
- Oily skin / humid climate (JETM preferred over WTF)
- WTF separation (normal, stir before use)
- WTF too dewy (use Tinted Face Powder to set)
- Competitor shade matching (agents prefer selfie over brand comparisons)

### 6. Response Time Issues
- January showed significant delays (5-20 days) with apologies for "influx of inquiries"
- By March, response times improved to ~2 days
- Delayed responses correlated with frustrated customers and refund requests

### 7. Seasonal Shade Flexibility
- Agents note that customers may need different shades in winter vs summer
- Example: Light in winter, Beige in summer if customer tans
- WTF recommended for dry winter skin, JETM for oily/warm-weather skin
