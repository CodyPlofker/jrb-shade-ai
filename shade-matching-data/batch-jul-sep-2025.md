## Key Patterns for AI Training

### Shade Mapping Matrix (from conversations)

| WTF/JETM | Face Pencil (Face) | Face Pencil (Eye) | Neutralizer | Sunscreen | Miracle Balm | Bronzer | Blush |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ivory | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Light | 7-8 | 4-6 | Fair Pink / Light Peachy Pink | Sandy | Dusty Rose, Au Naturel, Bronze, Sunkissed | Light Tan | Pop |
| Beige | 8-9 | 6-8 | Light Peachy Pink | Peachy | Dusty Rose, Bronze, Sunkissed, Flushed, Pinched Cheeks | Tan | Rosy |
| Medium-Honey | 13 | 11 | Medium Peach | Golden | Sunkissed, Pinched Cheeks | Medium Tan | Peachy |
| Golden | 17 | 15 | Dark Apricot | Apricot | Cocoa Bronze, Miami Beach | Terracotta | Peachy |

### Photo Quality Requirements (from agent rejections)
1. **No makeup** in the photo
2. **Natural lighting** -- either outside or indoors facing a window
3. **No shadows** on the face
4. **3-4 feet from a window** when indoors
5. **Chest up** framing
6. Photos with artificial/bathroom/overhead lighting are consistently rejected

### Agent Recommendation Structure (template)
1. Sunscreen shade recommendation
2. Face Pencil: two shades (face shade + under-eye shade, always 1-3 numbers apart)
3. Neutralizer Pencil (optional, for dark circles/spots)
4. Miracle Balm: two options (an all-over glow + a color/blush option)
5. WTF or JETM shade
6. Tinted Face Powder shade
7. Bronzer shade
8. Best Blush shade
9. Tools: Sharpener, Spatula, Skin Brush, Powder Brush

### Miracle Balm Usage Patterns
- **Au Naturel**: Clear, shimmer-free, moisture/glow, for all skin tones
- **Dusty Rose**: Cool-toned mauve glow, also acts as sheer bronzer for fair skin
- **Flushed**: True pink blush, shimmer-free, for all skin tones
- **Sunkissed**: Bronzed glow, neutralizes redness, deep sun-kissed finish
- **Bronze**: Sun-kissed warmth, deeper than Sunkissed
- **Pinched Cheeks**: Natural flush, for medium-to-warm tones
- **Cocoa Bronze**: For deeper skin tones
- **Miami Beach**: For deeper skin tones
- **Happy Hour / Golden Hour / Magic Hour**: All have shimmer/sparkle/glitter particles

### Cross-Brand Shade References
- Bobbi Brown Intensive Serum Foundation C-026 Cool Ivory -> JRB Beige WTF
- Clinique Sand (M) superbalanced foundation -> (customer asked, no agent mapping captured)

### Seasonal Variation
- Agents sometimes recommend a secondary shade for summer/tanning
- Example: Ivory primary, Light for summer months
