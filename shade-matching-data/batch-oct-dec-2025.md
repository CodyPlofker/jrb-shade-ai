## How Agents Shade Match

### Process
1. Customer sends a makeup-free selfie in natural light (facing a window, no shadows, chest up)
2. If photo quality is poor (bad lighting, shadows, makeup on), agent requests a new photo
3. Agent visually assesses skin tone from the photo
4. Agent provides a complete shade recommendation across multiple product categories
5. Agent explains product usage and application tips

### Recommendation Structure (Standard Template)
Agents follow a consistent template for full shade matches:
- **WTF / Just Enough Tinted Moisturizer**: Single shade recommendation
- **Face Pencil**: Two shades — one for face coverage, one (lighter) for under-eye
- **Neutralizer Pencil**: One or two shades — for color correcting under eyes/dark spots
- **Miracle Balm**: Multiple shades by use case (all-over glow, blush, bronzer, highlighter)
- **Additional products**: Tinted Face Powder, Bronzer, Blush, Sunscreen, Eyes, Lips

### Key Observations for AI Training

1. **Face Pencil Gap Rule**: The under-eye pencil is typically 1-2 shades lighter than the face pencil (e.g., Face 12 / Eye 11, Face 8 / Eye 6).

2. **Neutralizer Progression**: Fair Pink -> Light Peachy Pink -> Medium Peachy Pink -> Dark Peachy Pink. Secondary line: Fair Peach -> Light Peach -> Medium Peach -> Dark Apricot.

3. **Miracle Balm by Skin Depth**:
   - Fair/Light: Dusty Rose, Pinky Bronze, Bronze, Golden Hour
   - Medium: Dusty Rose/Bronze, Pinched Cheeks, Sunkissed, Happy Hour
   - Medium-Dark: Miami Beach, Sunkissed, Magic Hour
   - Golden/Dark: Bronze, Pinched Cheeks, Tawny, Magic Hour

4. **Au Naturel** is universally recommended across all skin tones as a clear/shimmer-free moisturizing glow option.

5. **Beige is the most common shade** -- it appeared in ~40% of recommendations. This is the "center" of the shade range for JRB's customer base.

6. **Cross-brand shade matching is discouraged** -- agents prefer selfie-based assessment over converting from other brands.

7. **Photo quality requirements**: Natural light, no makeup, facing window, no shadows, chest up. Agents will reject and re-request if lighting is poor.

8. **Sunscreen shade tracks WTF shade**: Fair/Beige -> Peachy; Medium -> Peachy; Golden -> Golden.

9. **Tinted Face Powder**: Light for Fair through Beige; Medium for Medium through Golden.

10. **Response templates are standardized** but shade selections are personalized per selfie. The boilerplate product descriptions are identical across tickets.
