Action Items

  1. Fix dark-bias in skin tone detection. 50% of users said "Too Dark." The AI prompt or shade-mapping logic needs to shift 1 shade lighter for Fair–Light-Medium range. This is the single highest-impact fix.
  2. Recalibrate WTF shade mapping. Only 10% accuracy. Even when skin tone is correct, the WTF shade can miss (e.g. Robin: Light tone correct, but wears Fair in WTF). The tone-to-shade mapping table may need an independent audit.
  3. Face Pencil mapping is unreliable. Errors go both too high and too low with no clear pattern. Likely needs a complete rework of the number assignment logic.
  4. Reduce Neutral-bias in undertone. Both undertone misses were AI saying "Neutral" when it should have been Cool or Warm. Consider a threshold adjustment or tie-breaking rule away from Neutral.
  5. Temper confidence scoring. 9 of 10 entries had "high" confidence, including clearly wrong results. Overconfidence erodes user trust.
  6. Expand demographic testing. All 10 respondents today are White/Caucasian. Need feedback from Medium, Medium-Dark, Dark, and Deep skin tones to validate across the full range.
  7. Deduplicate form submissions. Susan and Denise each submitted twice within ~2 minutes. Consider a confirmation screen or session-based dedup to prevent accidental doubles.
